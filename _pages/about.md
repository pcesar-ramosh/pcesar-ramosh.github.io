I am an economist from Bolivia and an M.Sc. candidate in Policy Economics at the [Center for Development Economics](https://cde.williams.edu/) (CDE), Williams College (2026–2027).

From 2020 to 2025 I worked as an econometric researcher in the Fiscal Analysis and Studies Unit of Bolivia's Ministry of Economy and Public Finance (MEFP), where I developed forecasting, nowcasting and scenario analysis to support fiscal and macroeconomic policy decisions. In 2025 I was the econometrics and machine-learning lead analyst for the Social Registry of Households Roadmap, a joint project of the MEFP and the Inter-American Development Bank (IDB).

I hold an M.Sc. in Economic Engineering from the [Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería](https://unifieecs.wixsite.com/msc-econ-engineering) (UNI, Peru) and a B.A. in Economics from the Universidad Mayor de San Andrés (UMSA, Bolivia). I am a member of the Heterogeneous Agents Research Initiative at the [Research Institute in Economics and Finance](https://ri-ef.com/) (RIEF), I serve as an ad hoc referee, and I have taught graduate courses in macroeconomics, econometrics, time series and machine learning for economics.

My research focuses on **Macroeconomics, Finance, and Microeconomics**, with a particular emphasis on:

- Heterogeneous agents in asset pricing and macroeconomics
- Interaction between fiscal and monetary policy
- Empirical macro-finance
- Informal labor markets
- Applied econometrics
- Machine learning applications in economics
