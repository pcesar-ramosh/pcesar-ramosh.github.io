### Center for Development Economics, Williams College (2026–2027)

- [ECON 501: Economic Growth](/notes/econ501/) (Fall 2026): PPP and Balassa–Samuelson, inequality and the Kuznets curve, growth arithmetic, the Solow model and poverty traps, the Malthusian model, the demographic transition and dividend, and income and happiness.

### M.Sc. in Economic Engineering, Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería (2024)

- [Heterogeneous Agents in Asset Pricing](/notes/ha-asset-pricing/) (Fall 2024, Professor Hamilton Galindo): stochastic processes and Itô calculus, dynamic programming and the HJB equation, the martingale approach, the Cox–Ingersoll–Ross general equilibrium model, finite-difference solutions with the upwind scheme, and a two-agent model with heterogeneous risk aversion. With diagrams, tables and figures.
