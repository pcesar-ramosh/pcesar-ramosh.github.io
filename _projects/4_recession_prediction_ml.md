**Paper:** Ramos, C. (2024). *Machine Learning Insights into Bolivia's Economic Downturns.* [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4981964)

Bolivia has no official recession predictor, and recessions are hard to forecast because they are rare and data are limited. This project applies Logistic Regression, Random Forests and Extreme Gradient Boosting to identify turning points in economic activity, integrating a broad set of data.

**Findings.** Real, monetary and fiscal variables are relevant for predicting recessions. The results add to the empirical literature and offer a starting point for an early-warning tool for policymakers.
